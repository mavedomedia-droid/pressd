FAQ

Quick answers.

Which areas do you cover?
Ilupeju, Gbagada, Yaba, Surulere, Ikeja and more on the Mainland. Ikoyi, Victoria Island and Lekki on the Island. You will see the full list when you book.
How does pickup work?
Pickup is the first step. Book it here and pay the pickup fee: ₦3,500 Mainland or ₦4,500 Island. That one fee covers pickup and delivery back to you. On the next pickup day, a rider collects your laundry. Add Pressd Load or Pressd Care while booking, or just send what you have ready.
What days do you do pickups?
Pickups run on Tuesdays, Thursdays, and Saturdays. You are scheduled for the next available day on that rotation.
How long until I get my clothes back?
Delivery is 48–72 hours after pickup. Larger orders and detailed Care items can sit at the longer end.
How does pricing work?
Pickup & delivery: ₦3,500 Mainland · ₦4,500 Island. One fee covers both ways.

Pressd Load: per-load pricing, see the pricing section above.

Pressd Care: per-item pricing with a ₦3,000 minimum tab. You see the full total before you pay.
Are the laundry services optional?
Yes. Pickup is the only required step. Add Pressd Load and/or Pressd Care while booking, or skip them and we bill you after we sort your laundry.
How do I get help?
Talk to Donut, our 24/7 support agent on Telegram: @pressdlagosbot. Or WhatsApp us. You can also track an order any time.
